Machine Catalog

Extraction
Miner (Mk.1 / Mk.2 / Mk.3)Extraction ▶

Automated ore extraction machine placed on resource nodes. Each tier doubles the output rate. The backbone of all raw material supply chains.

Stats
Mk.1 60/min (normal node)
Mk.2 120/min
Mk.3 240/min
Power 5 / 12 / 30 MW
Recipe (Mk.1)
Iron Plate + Concrete + Iron Rod + Cable → Miner Mk.1 — Equipment Workshop
Always place Miners on pure nodes first — pure nodes give 2x output automatically.
Water ExtractorExtraction ▶

Extracts water from any body of water. Must be placed directly on water surface. No node required — can be placed anywhere water exists. Outputs via pipe.

Stats
Output 120 m³/min
Power 20 MW
Recipe
Iron Plate + Iron Rod + Rotor + Cable → Water Extractor
Place multiple Extractors in large lakes for high-volume aluminium or nuclear production.
Oil ExtractorExtraction ▶

Extracts Crude Oil from oil resource nodes. Outputs via pipeline. Foundation of the petroleum and plastic production chains.

Stats
Output 60 m³/min (normal)
Power 40 MW
Recipe
Steel Pipe + Modular Frame + Motor + Computer → Oil Extractor
Build pipelines before placing extractors — oil needs immediate transport or it backs up.
Resource Well PressurizerExtraction ▶

Central unit placed on resource wells (nitrogen gas, etc). Activates all satellite extractors around the well. Required for nitrogen gas collection.

Stats
Power 150 MW
Requires Satellite extractors
Recipe
Heavy Modular Frame + Motor + Computer + Rubber → Well Pressurizer — Manufacturer
High power draw. Ensure sufficient power capacity before activating.
Production
SmelterProduction ▶

Basic smelting machine. Converts raw ore into ingots. Handles one input and one output. The first production machine unlocked. Always build in large numbers.

Stats
Inputs 1
Outputs 1
Power 4 MW
Recipe
Iron Rod + Wire + Concrete → Smelter
Pair one Miner Mk.1 with two Smelters on iron for a perfectly balanced early line.
FoundryProduction ▶

Advanced smelting machine handling two inputs. Used for alloy recipes like Steel Ingots (Iron + Coal). Larger and more powerful than the Smelter.

Stats
Inputs 2
Outputs 1
Power 16 MW
Recipe
Modular Frame + Rotor + Concrete → Foundry
Steel production is the primary use. Plan coal supply chains before building Foundries.
ConstructorProduction ▶

Single-input single-output manufacturing machine. Handles basic part construction (rods, plates, wire, etc). Your most-used machine in early and mid game.

Stats
Inputs 1
Outputs 1
Power 4 MW
Recipe
Iron Rod + Wire + Reinforced Iron Plate → Constructor
You will build hundreds of these. Automate their production early.
AssemblerProduction ▶

Two-input one-output production machine. Used for mid-game complex parts like Reinforced Iron Plates, Rotors, Circuit Boards, and Motors. Step up from Constructor.

Stats
Inputs 2
Outputs 1
Power 15 MW
Recipe
Reinforced Iron Plate + Rotor + Cable → Assembler
Most milestone parts require Assemblers. Plan factory layout for two belt inputs per machine.
ManufacturerProduction ▶

Four-input one-output production machine for complex late-game parts. Produces Heavy Modular Frames, Computers, Supercomputers, and more. High power draw.

Stats
Inputs 4
Outputs 1
Power 55 MW
Recipe
Motor + Heavy Modular Frame + Cable + Computer → Manufacturer
Managing four belt inputs requires careful factory layout planning. Allow extra space around each Manufacturer.
RefineryProduction ▶

Processes fluids and mixed fluid/solid recipes. Essential for all oil-based production (Plastic, Rubber, Fuel). Uses both conveyor belts and pipelines.

Stats
Inputs 1 belt + 1 pipe
Outputs 1 belt + 1 pipe
Power 30 MW
Recipe
Motor + Encased Industrial Beam + Steel Pipe + Computer → Refinery
Always process Heavy Oil Residue — leaving it unbottlenecked stops the whole refinery.
BlenderProduction ▶

Late-game fluid-mixing machine. Handles recipes requiring multiple fluids and solids simultaneously. Used for Cooling Systems, Batteries, Nitric Acid, and more.

Stats
Inputs 2 belts + 2 pipes
Outputs 1 belt + 1 pipe
Power 75 MW
Recipe
Motor + Heavy Modular Frame + Alclad Sheet + Computer → Blender
Essential for Turbo Motor and Battery production. Plan pipeline networks before building Blenders.
Particle AcceleratorProduction ▶

Endgame production machine for the most advanced recipes including Nuclear Pasta and Plutonium. Enormous power draw. Only a handful needed but they dominate your power budget.

Stats
Power 500-1500 MW
Tier Endgame
Recipe
Supercomputer + Electromagnetic Control Rod + Cooling System + Fused Modular Frame → Particle Accelerator
Each Particle Accelerator requires its own dedicated power infrastructure. Plan accordingly before construction.
Power
Biomass BurnerPower ▶

First power generator. Burns organic fuel (Solid Biofuel, Leaves, Wood). Must be manually refueled. Replace with Coal Generators as soon as possible.

Stats
Output 30 MW
Fuel Solid Biofuel / Biomass
Recipe
Iron Plate + Iron Rod + Wire → Biomass Burner
Build 5-6 immediately at game start. Then prioritize unlocking Coal Generators.
Coal GeneratorPower ▶

First automated power generator. Burns Coal and requires Water cooling. Fully automated — just supply coal and water continuously. Mid-game power backbone.

Stats
Output 75 MW
Coal 15/min
Water 45 m³/min
Recipe
Reinforced Iron Plate + Rotor + Cable + Concrete → Coal Generator
Build in clusters of 8-10. One Miner Mk.2 on a normal coal node feeds ~8 generators.
Fuel GeneratorPower ▶

Burns liquid fuel (Fuel, Turbofuel, Liquid Biofuel). Much higher power output than coal. Requires pipeline infrastructure. The preferred late-game power source before nuclear.

Stats
Output 150 MW (Fuel) / 300 MW (Turbofuel)
Fuel Via pipeline
Recipe
Modular Frame + Motor + Steel Pipe + Rubber → Fuel Generator
Turbofuel roughly doubles output per generator — worth the extra Sulfur investment.
Nuclear Power PlantPower ▶

Endgame power generator using Uranium or Plutonium Fuel Rods. Produces massive power but generates radioactive waste that must be processed or stored. Requires full hazmat infrastructure.

Stats
Output 2500 MW
Waste Uranium Waste / cycle
Recipe
Heavy Modular Frame + Computer + Alclad Sheet + Concrete → Nuclear Power Plant
Uranium Waste accumulates and must be processed. Build waste storage or Plutonium processing BEFORE activating reactors.
Geo Thermal GeneratorPower ▶

Placed on natural geysers. Generates free power with no fuel cost. Variable output depends on geyser quality. One of the most efficient power sources when geysers are available.

Stats
Output 100-400 MW (variable)
Fuel None
Recipe
Motor + Heavy Modular Frame + Cable + Steel Pipe → Geo Thermal Generator
Claim every geyser early. Free power locations are limited and extremely valuable.
Logistics
Conveyor Belt (Mk.1 — Mk.5)Logistics ▶

Primary item transport system. Each tier doubles throughput. The veins of your factory. Always upgrade belts when production bottlenecks occur.

Stats
Mk.1 60/min
Mk.2 120/min
Mk.3 270/min
Mk.4 480/min
Mk.5 780/min
If a belt is backing up, upgrade it or add a parallel line — never restrict input to the machine.
Splitter / MergerLogistics ▶

Splitters divide one belt into three outputs. Mergers combine up to three belts into one. Essential for load balancing and distributing materials across parallel production lines.

Stats
Splitter 1 in → 3 out
Merger 3 in → 1 out
Recipe
Iron Plate + Iron Rod + Screw → Splitter / Merger
Use Smart Splitters later to filter specific items onto dedicated lines.
Pipeline (Mk.1 / Mk.2) + PumpLogistics ▶

Transports liquids and gases. Mk.2 has double throughput. Pipeline Pumps boost flow over long distances or uphill. Required for all fluid-based production chains.

Stats
Mk.1 300 m³/min
Mk.2 600 m³/min
Recipe (Mk.1)
Iron Plate + Copper Sheet → Pipeline Mk.1
Pipelines flow downhill for free. Use elevation strategically to reduce pump requirements.
Drone PortLogistics ▶

Automated drone transport hub. Drones carry items between two ports at high speed over any terrain. Ideal for transporting rare resources from remote locations.

Stats
Fuel Packaged Fuel (drone)
Range Unlimited
Recipe
Computer + Steel Pipe + Motor + Rubber → Drone Port
Use for bauxite or nitrogen from remote nodes — cheaper than building conveyors across the map.
Train Station / Locomotive / Freight CarLogistics ▶

Rail-based high-volume transport. Trains carry massive quantities of materials across the map on dedicated rail networks. Best long-distance bulk transport solution in the game.

Stats
Capacity Very high
Speed High
Fuel Solid Biofuel / Coal / Packaged Fuel
Recipe
Heavy Modular Frame + Motor + Computer + Steel Beam → Locomotive
Train collisions are destructive. Always use signals and dedicated single-direction lanes on busy routes.

AWESOME SinkSpecial ▶

Destroys any item fed into it and converts it to AWESOME points for the AWESOME Shop. Never overflows — the perfect overflow solution for any production line. FICSIT approved waste elimination.

Stats
Input Anything
Output AWESOME points
Power 30 MW
Recipe
Reinforced Iron Plate + Cable + Wire + Concrete → AWESOME Sink
Connect to every production line's overflow output. Earns shop points passively.
AWESOME ShopSpecial ▶

Spends AWESOME points earned from the Sink to purchase cosmetics, customization options, and special items. No production impact but essential for Pioneer morale.

Stats
Input AWESOME points
Power 0 MW
Recipe
Unlocked at HUB — no construction cost
Prioritize unlocking the AWESOME Sink early to start accumulating points passively.

Phases

Phase 2 Versatile Framework + AIF
Phase 3 Modular Engine + AIF
Phase 4 Thermal Engine + Nuclear Pasta + AIF
