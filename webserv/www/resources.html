Ores
Iron Ore Common ▶

The most abundant ore on MASSAGE-2(AB)b. Foundation of early-game production. Used in virtually every production chain from basic construction to advanced electronics.

Used In
Iron IngotIron RodIron Plate ScrewReinforced Iron PlateRotor Smart PlatingModular Frame
