Creature Field Guide

Passive Creatures
Manta Ray / Lizard Doeling Passive ▶

Peaceful flying and ground creatures found throughout the map. Will flee if approached. No threat to pioneers. Can be petted for morale purposes — FICSIT recommends against time waste.

Drops
None
Found In
  • Grass Fields — Lizard Doelings grazing in open areas
  • Northern Forest — Manta Rays gliding over canopy
  • Dune Desert — various passive fauna
Completely harmless. Ignore and continue production.
Space Rabbit Passive ▶

Small burrowing creature. Harmless. Often found near cave entrances. Sometimes drops small items when startled. FICSIT classifies it as non-priority fauna.

Drops
Beryl Nut (rare)
Found In
  • Grass Fields — burrows near rocks
  • Northern Forest — forest floor
Non-threatening. Resume operations.
Hostile Creatures
Hog (Small / Medium) Hostile ▶

Aggressive boar-like creatures that charge at pioneers on sight. Medium hogs hit harder and have more health. Territorial — will pursue until you leave their zone.

Stats
HP Small: 50 / Medium: 200
Damage Small: 10 / Medium: 25
Attack Charge
Drops
Hog ProteinAlien DNA Capsule
Found In
  • Grass Fields — common near resource nodes
  • Northern Forest — patrols around iron deposits
  • Rocky Desert — medium hogs more frequent
Xeno-Basher or Iron Rebar dispatches small hogs efficiently. Keep moving to avoid charge.
Large Hog / Alpha Hog Dangerous ▶

Massive territorial predator. The Alpha Hog is the apex version — significantly larger, faster, and more aggressive. Can one-shot an unarmored pioneer. Approach with extreme caution.

Stats
HP Large: 500 / Alpha: 1500
Damage Large: 50 / Alpha: 150
Attack Charge + Stomp
Drops
Hog ProteinAlien DNA CapsuleOrganic Data Capsule (Alpha)
Found In
  • Dune Desert — Alpha Hogs near pure nodes
  • Red Jungle — large hogs patrolling oil fields
  • Titan Forest — Alpha Hogs guard rare deposits
Do NOT engage Alpha Hog without Hazmat Suit and Rifle. Retreat is always an option — FICSIT values pioneer assets.
